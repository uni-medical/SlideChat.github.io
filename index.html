SlideChat

A multimodal AI assistant for whole-slide computational pathology

Understanding gigapixel pathology images through slide-level visual and language-based interaction.

Nature Cancer Paper CVPR 2025 Paper Code & Resources

Overview

Whole-slide understanding, grounded in pathology context.

Whole-slide images contain gigapixel-scale tissue context that is difficult to capture with patch-level analysis alone. SlideChat connects slide-level visual representations with language models to support pathology description, question answering, and multimodal reasoning.

The project brings together a whole-slide vision-language assistant, instruction-following data, and benchmarks for evaluating captioning and visual question answering in computational pathology.

Method

From a whole-slide image to language-based analysis.

SlideChat encodes local pathology regions, builds slide-level representations, and aligns them with a language model for instruction following.

Data & Benchmark

Resources for training and evaluation.

SlideInstruction supports pathology-oriented instruction following, while SlideBench evaluates whole-slide captioning and visual question answering.

SlideInstruction

Instruction data constructed from whole-slide images and pathology reports for descriptions and pathology-focused question answering.

SlideBench

A benchmark suite for assessing whole-slide captioning and VQA across microscopy, diagnosis, and clinical scenarios.

Examples

Pathology descriptions and question answering at the whole-slide level.
