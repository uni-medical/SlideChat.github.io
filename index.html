SlideChat

A Multimodal AI Assistant for Whole-Slide Computational Pathology

Nature Cancer Paper CVPR 2025 Paper GitHub

Overview

Whole-slide images (WSIs) contain rich histological information at gigapixel resolution, yet conventional patch-level approaches often struggle to capture the broader tissue context essential for comprehensive pathological interpretation. SlideChat addresses this challenge by integrating fine-grained morphological features, global slide-level representations, and natural-language understanding within a unified multimodal framework.

Designed for flexible whole-slide analysis, SlideChat supports a wide range of pathology tasks, including visual question answering, pathology report generation, and multi-turn conversations. The framework is complemented by SlideInstruction, a large-scale instruction-following dataset, and SlideBench, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating multimodal understanding across diverse cancer types and clinical scenarios.

Method

From Whole-Slide Images to Natural-Language Understanding

SlideChat integrates local histological features and global tissue context through a hierarchical vision-language architecture. Each WSI is divided into non-overlapping patches, which are processed by a patch-level vision encoder to extract fine-grained morphological representations. These representations are then aggregated by a slide-level vision encoder to capture relationships across tissue regions and model global histological context. A multimodal connector projects the resulting visual tokens into the embedding space of a pretrained large language model (LLM), enabling the model to generate context-aware responses to natural-language queries. Question-to-image attention maps further visualize the tissue regions emphasized for specific questions.

Training Strategy. SlideChat adopts a two-stage training strategy consisting of cross-domain alignment and visual instruction learning. In the first stage, the slide-level encoder and connector are trained on 8,583 WSI–caption pairs to align visual representations with pathology descriptions, while the patch-level encoder and LLM remain frozen. In the second stage, the slide-level encoder, connector, and LLM are further optimized using 265,650 WSI question–answer pairs, enabling diverse capabilities such as visual question answering, pathology report generation, and multi-turn conversation.

Data

Slide-Level Instruction Dataset

SlideInstruction

SlideInstruction is a large-scale multimodal instruction dataset developed to support whole-slide pathology understanding. Curated from 8,583 WSIs and their associated pathology reports, it comprises 274,233 training samples spanning 24 anatomical sites and 31 cancer types.

The dataset covers diverse instruction formats, including report generation, binary judgment, multiple-choice questions, short- and long-form visual question answering, and multi-turn conversations, providing comprehensive supervision for language-driven whole-slide analysis.
