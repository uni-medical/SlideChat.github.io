SlideChat

A multimodal AI assistant for whole-slide computational pathology

Understanding gigapixel pathology images through slide-level visual and language-based interaction.

Nature Cancer Paper CVPR 2025 Paper GitHub

Overview

Whole-slide understanding, grounded in pathology context.

Whole-slide images contain gigapixel-scale tissue context that is difficult to capture with patch-level analysis alone. SlideChat connects slide-level visual representations with language models to support pathology description, question answering, and multimodal reasoning.

The project brings together a whole-slide vision-language assistant, instruction-following data, and benchmarks for evaluating captioning and visual question answering in computational pathology.

Method

From a whole-slide image to language-based analysis.

SlideChat encodes local pathology regions, builds slide-level representations, and aligns them with a language model for clinical queries and question-guided interpretability.

Data & Benchmark

Instruction data built for slide-level pathology.

SlideInstruction supports report generation, judgement, multiple-choice questions, visual question answering, and conversation over whole-slide pathology images.

Examples

Whole-slide pathology conversations.

Representative SlideChat responses to questions about tissue architecture, diagnosis, and histological features.
